Common questions

Before you call.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

An appointment is strongly recommended — procedure days fill up and walk-ins may face a long wait. The quickest way to get a slot is WhatsApp at +91 90922 32991; you will usually be confirmed the same day.

What does a consultation cost?

Consultation is charged separately from treatment and is discussed openly when you book. If you proceed with a procedure on the same day, the consultation fee is adjusted against it.

Will Dr. Kavitha do the procedure herself?

Yes — every time. Lasers, injectables, minor surgery and hair procedures are performed by Dr. Kavitha personally. Support staff assist with preparation and aftercare, not with the procedure itself.

Are these treatments safe for darker Indian skin?

They are — provided the device settings are right. Deeper skin types are more prone to post-inflammatory pigmentation, which is why device parameters, peel strengths and session intervals at Skin Trust are calibrated specifically for Tamil Nadu skin rather than copied from a manufacturer's default.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends entirely on the concern. Hair PRP is typically a course over several months; acne scars usually need multiple combined sessions; laser hair removal takes six to eight sittings; a mole removal is often one visit. The expected number is always given to you in writing at the consultation.

Do you offer EMI or instalment payments?

Multi-session courses can be paid in instalments. We do not push prepaid packages for treatments you may not need — the plan is reviewed at intervals and adjusted based on your response.

Do you offer laser hair removal for full body?

Yes. We use a medical-grade Diode Laser which is safe for all Indian skin types. Treatment areas include face, upper lip, underarms, arms, legs, bikini line and full body. Each session is performed by Dr. Kavitha.
